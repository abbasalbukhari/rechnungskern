Frequently asked questions

What is an EN 16931 e-invoice?

An invoice in a structured, machine-readable format according to the European standard EN 16931. In Germany the common formats are XRechnung (pure XML) and ZUGFeRD/Factur-X (PDF with embedded XML). A plain PDF is not an e-invoice.

What is the difference between ZUGFeRD and XRechnung?

ZUGFeRD/Factur-X is a hybrid PDF/A-3 with a readable document and embedded XML data – ideal for business customers. XRechnung is the standard for invoices to German public authorities: XML only, with additional mandatory fields such as the Leitweg-ID.

When does e-invoicing become mandatory in Germany?

Since 1 January 2025 all businesses must be able to receive e-invoices. Sending becomes mandatory on 1 January 2027 for companies with more than EUR 800,000 previous-year turnover and on 1 January 2028 for all B2B businesses.

Is the service really free?

Yes. Creating and validating in the browser is free without registration. For the API a short e-mail is enough to get a key. There is no guarantee of availability; the service may be restricted or discontinued at any time.

Is my invoice data stored?

No. Data is processed in memory only and discarded immediately after delivery – no copies, no backups, no analytics. The editor stores your entries exclusively in your own browser. Details in the privacy policy.
